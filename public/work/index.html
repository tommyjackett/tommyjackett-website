Work with me

I make video and podcasts through VIDPOD, from the first idea to the final edit. If you’ve got a project coming up, get in touch.

Get in touchGo to VIDPOD

01VIDPOD

VIDPOD is a video and podcast production company in Melbourne. It’s the same job as the stories on this site: work out what the interesting bit is, then shoot it so people can see it.

Go to VIDPODThe VIDPOD story page

02What I make

On the video side that’s brand films, founder stories, case studies and testimonials, explainers, social content and animation.

On the podcast side it’s recording, editing and launching shows: branded podcasts, educational ones, internal ones, video podcasts and remote recording.

03Shows

I know what it takes to keep a show going. Josh Janssen and I made a podcast every day for three and a half years.

Shows made through VIDPOD include:

  • The Numbers Game
  • Let’s Talk Teachingfor Monash
  • Unconventional Businessfor HubSpot
  • The Huddlefor Help at Hand Education
  • Home Filesfor Carlisle Homes

04How it goes

Here’s how it usually goes. You tell me what you’re after, even roughly. I ask a few questions, then send you a plan and a quote in writing. We shoot or record, I edit, and you tell me what’s off until it’s right.
