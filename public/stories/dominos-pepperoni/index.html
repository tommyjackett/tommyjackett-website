Domino’s promised 100 slices of pepperoni

By Tommy Jackett. Published 16 March 2023. Updated 19 October 2026.

53

slices of pepperoni, counted on camera. The promise was 100.

Play: Domino’s Promise 100 Pieces Of Pepperoni, So I Counted Them!

Domino’s Promise 100 Pieces Of Pepperoni, So I Counted Them!A video by Tommy Jackett. Watch on YouTube

Domino’s said its Pepperoni Deluxe was topped right to the edge with 100 slices of pepperoni. So I counted them.

I counted them on camera. The total was 53. “That’s all 53 slices of pepperoni on the pepperoni Deluxe at Domino’s where they promise 100 slices of pepperoni. Case closed.”

“That’s all 53 slices of pepperoni on the pepperoni Deluxe at Domino’s where they promise 100 slices of pepperoni. Case closed.”

It’s not the first time I’ve counted something to check a claim. In 2016 I counted the steps at the 1000 Steps in Victoria. My count was 776, and Pedestrian TV ran the story.

I posted the pepperoni video in March 2023. It’s a minute and a half.
