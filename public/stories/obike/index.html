Fishing for oBikes in the Yarra

By Tommy Jackett. Published 23 September 2017. Updated 19 October 2026.

In September 2017 oBikes were everywhere in Melbourne, including the bottom of the Yarra.

I got a bit obsessed. Not with the bikes. With how everyone reacted to them. So I grabbed a hook and went fishing. I pulled out seven and put the video up.

Pedestrian TV ran it. ABC Radio Melbourne had me on Melbourne Mornings to talk about it. Within days oBike had contractors out on the river, and they pulled out more than 40 bikes.

Then it got a bit silly. I got some t-shirts printed and [[NEEDS TOMMY: the Sunrise detail, who got in touch and whether you wore one on air]]. The shirts made $1,520 [[NEEDS TOMMY: where the money went]].

In the last video I thank oBike for having bikes absolutely everywhere, and ask them to go clean them up.

In 2022 I did it again, with e-scooters. Five out of the Yarra in under two hours, and 3AW had me on with Neil Mitchell.

The first two videos have been watched more than 100,000 times between them.
