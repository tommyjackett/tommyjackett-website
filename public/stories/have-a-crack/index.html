Have A Crack

By Tommy Jackett. Published 29 March 2023. Updated 19 October 2026.

“It doesn’t always unfold as you may have hoped, but the success is found in the action of giving it a crack.”

I’ve been having a crack for years.

In 2023 I made a video about it. The idea is simple. “It doesn’t always unfold as you may have hoped, but the success is found in the action of giving it a crack.”

Here’s my proof. If I’d never had a crack at making a web series in America, I don’t think I ever would have found my love for filmmaking. I’ve hosted breakfast radio in Shepparton. I’ve fished bikes out of the Yarra. Some of it worked, plenty of it didn’t, and every bit of it led to the next thing.

It’s all about finding success one crack at a time, and it really sums up my approach. If you’re feeling unsure or stuck or don’t know where to start, just get out there and have a crack.
