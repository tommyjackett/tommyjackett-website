The Jackett Ranch

By Tommy Jackett. Published 6 October 2026. Updated 19 October 2026.

Play: Driving Across America to Find My Family’s Forgotten Land

Part 1 Driving Across America to Find My Family’s Forgotten LandA video by Tommy Jackett. Watch on YouTube

Imagine if you could buy a block of land. 8.5 acres, $5,000, no deposit, $50 a week.

That’s exactly what my dad did in the ’70s. A block in Colorado, paid off over 10 years, and the deed turned up in 1985. He has never seen it.

So I found a mate who was down for a road trip. A 15-hour flight from Melbourne to San Francisco, 2,500 kilometres of driving, all to see if my family was sitting on a gold mine. The local real estate agent took us out in his Jeep, because our hire car wouldn’t have made it.

It was a steep hill. No water, no electricity, and it snows about half the year.

The block was one of nearly 8,000 cut from a huge ranch. Thousands of people bought them, and most are still empty. As I say in the film, the Forbes family are up $175 million and the Jacketts are down $5K, plus the taxes each year.

Dad’s cutting his losses. He didn’t think anyone would want it, so I put it on the internet and let the internet do what it does best. At the end of Part 1 I offered it up for free.
