In Guns We Trust is free this Friday

By Tommy Jackett

Published 19 October 2026

In Guns We Trust is free on YouTube this Thursday at 8pm in New York. That’s Friday 23 October, 11am in Melbourne. It runs 76 minutes, and you can watch it anywhere in the world.

Josh Janssen directed it and I’m in it with him. We love America, and the one thing neither of us ever understood was the guns. We didn’t have a position. We figured that might be the point.

So we went with cameras and one rule: no side, no agenda, just conversations. More than thirty people speak for themselves in it, a Hollywood armourer, a Route 91 survivor, a Texas sheriff and a trauma surgeon among them.

Early on I introduce myself to the armourer. I tell him: “My gun experience is that I watch a lot of movies, so I know what I see, but I don’t know physically, because I don’t have access to them like that.” That’s about where I started.

It’s Josh’s first feature and my first long-form film, and on Friday it’s out in the world.

The film includes gunfire and talk of shootings, suicide and loss. It’s made for adults and older teenagers watching with support. If you need someone to talk to, call or text 988 in the US, call Lifeline on 13 11 14 in Australia, or find a line near you at findahelpline.com.

Watch it. Share it. Talk about it.
