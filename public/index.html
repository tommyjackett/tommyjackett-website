About Tommy Jackett

I’m a creator who has been immersed in video, podcasts and content production for over 10 years. I started out on breakfast radio in Shepparton and made more than 1,000 episodes of The Daily Talk Show with Josh. What drives me is the spark: a story, a person or an idea I can’t stop thinking about, and then going all the way down the rabbit hole. In Guns We Trust is the biggest one yet, a feature documentary where Josh and I dove into one of America’s most divided topics.

I also run VIDPOD, a Melbourne video and podcast production company. We make brand films, social content and podcasts for businesses, including shows for Monash, HubSpot and Carlisle Homes.
