About Tommy Jackett

I’m a creator who has been immersed in video, podcasts and content production for over 10 years. I started out on breakfast radio in Shepparton, made more than 1,000 episodes of The Daily Talk Show with Josh, and went fishing for oBikes in the Yarra. These days I make my own series, and with Josh, a feature documentary.
