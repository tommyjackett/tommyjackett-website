01On now

In Guns We Trust

In Guns We Trust is a documentary Josh Janssen directed, and I’m in it with him. We’re two unarmed Australian mates who didn’t have a position on guns, so we crossed America with cameras and listened to the people who do. A Hollywood armourer, a Route 91 survivor, a Texas sheriff, a trauma surgeon. Everyone speaks for themselves. No side, no agenda, just conversations.

76 minutes. Free, worldwide, on YouTube.

Free on YouTube, worldwide. Thursday 22 October, 8pm ET. That’s Friday 23 October, 11am in Melbourne (AEDT).
