In Guns We Trust

I’m in a documentary called In Guns We Trust, and you can watch it free.

In Guns We Trust is free on YouTube, worldwide, from Thursday 22 October 2026 at 8pm US Eastern, which is Friday 23 October at 11am in Melbourne (AEDT). It runs 76 minutes. Josh Janssen directed it and I’m in it with him.

01The film

Josh and I love America. The food, the films, the bigger-is-better attitude. The one thing we’ve never understood is the guns. Australia rarely has to think about them, so neither of us had a position. We decided that might be the point.

So we crossed the country with cameras and one rule: no side, no agenda, just conversations. A Hollywood armourer, a Route 91 survivor, a Texas sheriff, a trauma surgeon, and a lot of people in between. More than thirty people speak for themselves in it. Josh and I do the asking.

Here’s roughly where I started. Early on I say:

“I feel like, from where we’re from, we’re not thinking about guns, so I feel like I’m neutral at this moment.”

The film doesn’t tell you what to think. It’s made to be talked about afterwards.

02My part

I’m featured in the film with Josh and credited as co-host and story by. On camera I’m the outsider, and I say so.

When I meet the armourer I tell him:

“My gun experience is that I watch a lot of movies, so I know what I see, but I don’t know physically, because I don’t have access to them like that.”

It’s Josh’s first feature and my first long-form film. He directed and I’m in it with him. Each of the people we met speaks for themselves, and we left them to.

05Questions

Questions people ask

Who is Tommy Jackett?

Tommy Jackett is a Melbourne video producer, storyteller and podcast host. He is featured in and co-hosts the 2026 documentary In Guns We Trust, directed by Josh Janssen, and makes video and podcasts for businesses and creators through VIDPOD.

When and where can I watch In Guns We Trust?

It’s free on YouTube, worldwide, on the official channel @InGunsWeTrustFilm, from Thursday 22 October 2026 at 8pm ET (Friday 23 October, 11am AEDT). There’s no paywall and no registration, and YouTube may ask some viewers to sign in to confirm their age. It isn’t on Netflix, Prime or Stan. We wanted as many people as possible to see it, so it’s free.

What was Tommy Jackett’s part in the film?

I’m featured in it alongside Josh Janssen, and I’m credited as co-host and story by. On screen I’m one of the two Australians asking the questions.

Who directed and made it?

Josh Janssen directed it. He’s a Melbourne documentary filmmaker and the founder of Full Stack Films. It was produced by Jess Lucas and Breana Phillips for 2A Film Pty Ltd, Full Stack Films and Rethink Films, and it runs for 76 minutes.

Is it for or against guns?

Neither. Josh and I set ourselves one rule: no side, no agenda, just conversations. Gun owners, survivors, a sheriff, researchers and families each speak for themselves. The aim isn’t to change anyone’s mind. It’s to show that people who disagree can still sit down, ask honest questions and hear each other.

Why two Australians?

We came as outsiders, from a country where guns rarely come up in daily life. Most of what Australians hear about guns in America arrives through news of mass shootings. Those stories are real, and the film doesn’t look away from them, but they leave out most of the people who live with guns every day.

Is it suitable for students or children?

The film includes gunfire and conversations about mass shootings, suicide and loss. It’s made for adults and older teenagers watching with support. Educators should preview it first and consider their group’s needs.

Can I host a screening?

Yes, and we’d love you to. Anyone can host a free screening at home, on campus, at a library or in a community group. The discussion guide is free to print. Email screenings@ingunswetrustfilm.com, or see the resources page on the film’s site.
