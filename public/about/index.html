Hey, I’m Tommy. Tommy Jackett, filmmaker and storyteller

I started on breakfast radio in Shepparton. Then I had a go at TV presenting, and ended up on The Project on Network 10.

Then YouTube. My favourite videos were always something small that everyone had noticed. Why are there oBikes at the bottom of the Yarra? Does a $3 coffee fit in a $2 cup? So I went and checked.

Josh Janssen and I made a podcast every day for three and a half years. The Daily Talk Show, more than 1,000 episodes.

Now I’m making my own stories. The Jackett Ranch, DJ Bodhi, and a feature documentary, In Guns We Trust, which Josh and I made across America with one rule: no side, no agenda, just conversations. I still run VIDPOD, a video production company, and I’m a dad.

  1. 2013Breakfast radio in Shepparton. Two years.
  2. 2015Made videos about people, a man who lives in a caravan so he can help others, and a surfer in Sydney Harbour.
  3. 2016I counted the steps at the 1000 Steps. Pedestrian TV ran it.
  4. 2017oBikes in the Yarra. ABC Radio Melbourne had me on.
  5. 2018The Daily Talk Show starts. Then a 7-Eleven coffee cup.
  6. 2021The Daily Talk Show wraps up, more than 1,000 episodes in.
  7. 2022Tomversation, a podcast of conversations. The e-scooters, and 3AW.
  8. 2023Counted Domino’s pepperoni. Made Have A Crack. Backed Bodhi’s DJ dream and met Will Sparks.
  9. 2024Josh and I cross America with a camera crew.
  10. 2026In Guns We Trust, free on YouTube. The Jackett Ranch.
