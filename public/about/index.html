Hey, I’m Tommy.

I live in Melbourne, I make video, and I can’t resist a rabbit hole.

I’ve been at it for more than a decade. I hosted breakfast radio in Shepparton. I fished abandoned oBikes out of the Yarra, tipped a $3 coffee into a $2 cup and counted the pepperoni on a Domino’s pizza. For three and a half years Josh Janssen and I made a podcast every day, more than 1,000 episodes of it. Plenty of what I’ve tried didn’t work. I’m fine with that.

These days I make video and podcasts through VIDPOD, and I make my own stories on the side. The biggest so far is In Guns We Trust, a documentary Josh directed and I’m in. It’s free on YouTube, worldwide, from Thursday 22 October 2026 at 8pm US Eastern, which is Friday 23 October at 11am in Melbourne (AEDT).

If something here catches your eye, have a look around. If you’ve got a project coming up, get in touch.

01The short version

Tommy Jackett is a Melbourne video producer, storyteller and podcast host. He is featured in and co-hosts the 2026 documentary In Guns We Trust, directed by Josh Janssen, and makes video and podcasts for businesses and creators through VIDPOD.

02Questions people ask

Where is Tommy Jackett based?

I’m in Melbourne, Australia.

What has Tommy Jackett made?

The documentary In Guns We Trust, which I’m in with director Josh Janssen. A run of online videos, including fishing abandoned oBikes out of the Yarra, the 7-Eleven coffee cup test and the Domino’s pepperoni count. And more than 1,000 episodes of The Daily Talk Show with Josh.

How do I hire Tommy Jackett for video or podcast production?

Through VIDPOD, the production company I make video and podcasts with. Use the contact form and tell me what you’ve got coming up.

How do I contact Tommy Jackett?

Use the form on the contact page, or email me directly. For press about the film, the film’s own press kit has the right contacts.
